An entry for the [make.fun best heist game bounty](https://make.fun/c/best-heist-game-20261122-0559).

**Velvet Vault** is a top-down 2D heist game that runs entirely offline in a
desktop browser. Slip through the night museum, read the guards' patrols and
vision cones, dodge the sweeping security cameras, crack the vault, pocket the
Aurora Emerald and 24 treasures, and reach the getaway door before the alarm
brings the whole house down on you.

One polished level: 8 patrolling guards with learnable routes, 4 sweeping
cameras, a suspicion meter that gives you a chance to recover, sneak movement,
shadow hiding spots, and 3 gadgets — a thrown coin to lure guards away, a
smoke bomb to break line of sight, and a lockpick for the vault.

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser and play with keyboard and mouse:
WASD / arrows to move, Shift to sneak, E to grab loot and crack the vault,
F to toss a coin, Q for a smoke bomb, M to mute, R to restart.
